> **개요 (한국어):** 서사 일지. 사람이나 Claude가 손으로 쓴다. 어떤 훅도 이 폴더를 쓰지 않는다.

> **What lands here:** A longer, narrative account of a session — the story of what happened, not the atomic decisions/mistakes/patterns extracted from it.
> **Who writes it:** A human, or Claude on request — never a hook.
> **When:** Whenever a session is worth a durable write-up in its own words.

This folder is empty on purpose. Your first session fills it — or it may stay empty indefinitely, and that is fine too.

Unlike every other runtime folder, no automation touches `Sessions/`: `SessionEnd` distills into `Decisions/`, `Mistakes/`, and `Patterns/`, but it never writes a narrative entry. If you want a session remembered as a story rather than as extracted atoms, ask Claude to write one here, or write it yourself. Name files `YYYY-MM-DD-<kebab-slug>.md` per [`_System/conventions.md`](../_System/conventions.md).
