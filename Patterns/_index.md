> **개요 (한국어):** 다음에도 쓸 수 있는 재사용 가능한 접근법을 SessionEnd 증류 훅이 자동으로 기록한다.

> **What lands here:** An approach that worked and is likely reusable in a different session — a way of structuring a task, not a one-off fact.
> **Who writes it:** The SessionEnd distill hook (`distill-session.py`).
> **When:** Automatically, when a session ends — no manual step.

This folder is empty on purpose. Your first session fills it.

Patterns differ from `Knowledge/` in origin, not content: a pattern starts as a raw, unreviewed observation the hook pulled straight out of a session transcript, while `Knowledge/` holds how-tos Claude deliberately wrote and curated. A pattern that keeps recurring is usually worth promoting into a proper `Knowledge/` page; until then it lives here as a dated fragment. See [`_System/stop-hook.md`](../_System/stop-hook.md) and [`_System/consolidate.md`](../_System/consolidate.md) for how fragments eventually get folded into topic pages.
